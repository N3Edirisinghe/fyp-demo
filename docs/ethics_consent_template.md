# Informed Consent Template (Draft — for Ethics Committee Review)

> This is a starting draft only. Submit to the SLTC Ethics Review Committee
> for review and required amendments before use with any participant.

## Study Title
Designing an Emotionally Adaptive AI Companion Using Voice Emotion
Recognition and Long-Term Emotional Memory

## Purpose
You are invited to participate in a research study evaluating an AI
companion system that responds to your voice and, in some sessions,
remembers previous conversations. The study investigates whether these
features improve emotion recognition accuracy, personalization, trust,
companionship, and overall satisfaction compared to conventional AI
assistants.

## What You Will Be Asked to Do
- Attend two sessions (approximately one week apart).
- Speak to an AI companion via your device's microphone.
- Self-report your emotional state after some interactions.
- Complete short surveys after each session.
- Optionally interact with a plain text-only chatbot for comparison.

## Data We Will Collect
- Transcripts of what you say to the system (not raw audio, unless
  separately and explicitly consented to).
- Emotion labels detected by the system, and your own self-reported
  emotion labels.
- Your survey responses.
- No data is linked to your real name — you will be assigned an
  anonymized participant ID.

## Data We Will NOT Collect
- Your real name or other directly identifying information.
- Raw audio recordings, by default (see `STORE_RAW_AUDIO` setting).
- Any data from your device beyond what you say during study sessions.

## Your Rights
- Participation is voluntary. You may withdraw at any time without penalty.
- You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting the
  research team at: [insert contact email].
- Your data will be stored securely and used only for this research.
- This system is a research prototype and is **not** a substitute for
  professional mental health support. If you are experiencing distress,
  please contact [insert local counselling/crisis service contact].
