Terms and Conditions

AffetX Emotionally Adaptive Companion Study Agreement

1. Introduction

By interacting with the AffetX Emotionally Adaptive AI Companion ("AffetX"), you agree to participate in this academic evaluation study. This study investigates multimodal voice and text emotion recognition combined with empathetic conversational dynamics.

2. Accepting this Agreement

In order to participate, you must first agree to these Terms. You may not proceed with the study if you do not accept these Terms. Your participation confirms that you are of legal age to provide voluntary research consent.

3. Voice Acoustics and Semantic Analysis

During the session, your spoken audio is processed in real time to classify acoustic pitch harmonics (CNN-LSTM) and lexical sentiment (RoBERTa). All acoustic feature extraction is ephemeral and evaluated on-device or within transient memory solely for conversational response synthesis.

4. Privacy, Anonymity & Data Protection

We strictly protect your privacy. We do not collect, harvest, or store any personally identifiable information (PII), including your real name, email address, IP address, or location. All session logs and evaluation metrics are completely anonymized and assigned randomized session IDs.

5. Voluntary Participation & Right to Withdraw

Your participation in this research is entirely voluntary. You reserve the right to terminate your conversation and withdraw from the study at any moment by clicking the exit icon (✕) without any penalty.

6. General Terms

The system is provided for academic evaluation and supportive conversational companionship. Data collected from this session will be used exclusively for research analysis, model evaluation, and academic publications.
