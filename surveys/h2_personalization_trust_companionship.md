# H2 Survey: Personalization, Trust, and Companionship

Administered at the end of **Session 1** (baseline, memory disabled) and
**Session 2** (post, memory enabled), using identical items so scores are
directly comparable in a paired analysis (Section 5.5).

**Scale:** 1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree

## Personalization
1. The companion's responses felt tailored to me specifically.
2. The companion seemed to understand my individual situation.

## Trust
3. I trust the companion's responses to be genuinely supportive.
4. I would feel comfortable sharing something personal with this companion.

## Companionship
5. I felt a sense of companionship during this interaction.
6. Talking with the companion felt similar to talking with someone who knows me.

## Administration Notes
- Present items in the same order across both sessions.
- Do not tell participants which session is the "memory" condition — avoid
  demand characteristics by keeping the framing neutral ("please rate your
  experience today").
